What's the best time to visit Singapore?

Year-round warm weather (75-90°F). Avoid monsoon months (November-January) for better weather. All months offer excellent luxury shopping and dining.

How much does a luxury Singapore stay cost?

Five-star hotels run $400-$800+ per night; Michelin dining $150-$300 per person. A 4-5 day luxury trip averages $3,000-$6,000 per person.
