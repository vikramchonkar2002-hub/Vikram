# STYLE.md — default clean modern style (no reference reels supplied)

## Captions
- 2–3 words on screen at once, bold white sans-serif (Montserrat/Inter Black feel), 6px dark outline + soft shadow.
- Active word in one accent colour: Fora gold #F2C14E (fits the luxury-travel brand).
- Lower third: caption baseline ~y=1380 of 1920 — under the face, above the 400px bottom safe zone. Never over the face.
- Text never in the top 250px or bottom 400px (Instagram UI).

## Motion
- Hard jump cuts, alternating 100% / ~110% punch-in on every cut, zoom centred on the face so it stays fully in frame.
- A new visual every 2–4 s (punch-in change, pop-up text, number, logo).
- First 2 s: strongest visual — big hook text + punch-in, no slow start.

## Overlays
- Every concrete thing mentioned (number, app, product, step) gets a pop-up at that moment, ≥1.2 s on screen, in the upper-middle band (y 260–520) clear of the face where possible.

## Audio
- Loudness -14 LUFS integrated, true peak -1 dBTP. No music.
- Never colour-grade or alter skin tone.

## Footage notes (Vikram, Oct 2026 batch)
- Source: 720x1280 24fps phone clips, 8 s each, five locations (desk w/ blue wall, hotel lobby, notebook, phone stats, advice).
- Face sits at ~y 25–50% of frame (centre-upper); lower-third captions are clear of it. Hands gesture into the lower third — captions may overlap hands, which is fine.
- Raw loudness -14.3 to -16.4 LUFS; needs per-clip normalisation so clips match.
