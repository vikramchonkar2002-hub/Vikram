Bali Travel Planner's Guide

What's the best time to visit Bali?

April to October is dry season—perfect for sun and water activities. July-August is peak (busier), while April-May and September-October offer great weather and fewer crowds. November-March is monsoon season with discounts but rain. Your advisor can recommend specific weeks based on your preferences.

How much does a luxury Bali vacation cost?

Budget $3,000–$5,000 per person per week at a five-star resort with meals; $8,000+ per night for a private villa with chef. Booking through Suvan Fora costs the same as booking direct but includes upgrades, credits, and access to exclusive properties.

How many days should I spend in Bali?

5–7 days lets you explore a region fully (Seminyak beach + Ubud culture, or just one area deeply). 10–14 days is ideal for a honeymoon or anniversary trip, mixing relaxation with activities and a day trip or two.
