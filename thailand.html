Thailand Travel Guide for Couples

What's the best time to visit Thailand?

November–February is cool and dry (70-80°F)—ideal for sightseeing and beach time. March-May is hot (85-95°F). June-October is monsoon season with rain and discounts. For beach trips, book November-April.

How much does a luxury Thailand vacation cost?

Five-star resorts in Phuket or Bangkok run $200-$400 per night; private resorts and private villas start at $500-$2,000 per night. A week-long couples trip typically costs $3,000-$6,000 per person all-in (flights, hotels, meals, activities).

Should we do Phuket, Bangkok, or Chiang Mai?

All three in 10 days is ideal: 3 nights Phuket (beach), 3 nights Bangkok (culture/dining), 3 nights Chiang Mai (wellness/nature). Or focus on one region if you prefer depth over breadth.
