Frequently Asked Questions

What does a luxury travel advisor do?

A luxury travel advisor designs and books bespoke vacations on your behalf — curating private villas, five-star resorts, and exclusive experiences, then handling logistics, upgrades, and on-trip support. Suvan Fora specializes in serving HNI and UHNI clients who want a fully personalized itinerary without doing the planning themselves.

Does it cost more to book luxury travel through an advisor?

No. Booking through Suvan Fora typically costs the same as booking directly with a hotel or resort, while adding complimentary perks such as room upgrades, resort credit, early check-in, and late checkout at partner properties.

What is Fora Travel?

Fora Travel is a modern travel agency that combines expert human advisors with technology to plan and book trips. Fora advisors have collectively booked billions of dollars in travel across 180+ countries and maintain preferred relationships with luxury hotel and resort brands worldwide.

How do I book a luxury vacation with Suvan Fora?

Fill out the trip request form below with your destination, travel dates, and preferences, or schedule a complimentary consultation call. Suvan Fora will follow up with a tailored itinerary and next steps.

Do you work with high-net-worth and ultra-high-net-worth clients?

Yes. Suvan Fora focuses on HNI and UHNI travelers seeking privacy, exceptional service, and access to exclusive properties and experiences, including private villas, overwater residences, safari lodges, and yacht or private jet coordination.
